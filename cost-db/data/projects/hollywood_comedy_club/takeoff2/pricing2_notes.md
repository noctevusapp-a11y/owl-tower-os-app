# Hollywood Comedy Club TI - book pricing of the takeoff2 files (Los Angeles, latest index month)

Internal working document, prepared 2026-10-28 for the Owl Tower estimating file. Price source: `cost-db/data/books/rsmeans_bcc_2022/book_lines_la_latest.csv`, rows with `status = verified` only, columns `*_la_latest` (period 2026-Q02). The same rules as the first estimate (`scripts/price.py`, `summary.md` sections 1 and 6) were applied: a line is priced only from a verified row, machine_read / flagged rows are reported as candidates with the price empty, no allowances, no percentages, no tolerances, no second location or time factor, every unit conversion written out with its source, bare M / L / E kept separate, the book O&P column reported for reference only and never added. Nothing was sent to anyone; no file outside `takeoff2/` was written.

Files: `scripts/price2.py` (engine, usage `python3 -I price2.py <takeoff.csv> <matches.csv> <book.csv> <out_priced.csv>`), `scripts/build_matches2.py` (writes the three mapping files `matches_mep.csv`, `matches_equipment.csv`, `matches_demo_struct_gc.csv`), `scripts/summarize2.py` (this file). Outputs `priced_mep.csv`, `priced_equipment.csv`, `priced_demo_struct_gc.csv` carry the columns of `../estimate_la_latest.csv` plus `spec_or_model`, `furnished_by`, `installed_by`. `arch.csv` was not priced (being produced by another agent); run `price2.py arch.csv matches_arch.csv <book> priced_arch.csv` with its own mapping file when it is ready.

**Tiers.** `priced_exact`: the verified row is the specified work and the product / size / type as the documents fix it (same unit or an exact stated conversion). `priced_substitute`: same kind of work, product / size / type not fixed or differing (the exact difference is in `reason`; `match` = substitute, or partial when the row covers part of the component). `book_row_not_matching`: the only candidate row is a different kind of work. `book_row_not_verified`: the matching row is machine_read / flagged (or sits under an unreadable section header without a cost code). `not_in_book`, `not_quantified` (takeoff qty empty or the dimension needed for the book unit is not on the documents), `spec_not_issued` (quantity on hand but the product / construction needed to choose a row is not stated), `owner_provided` / `existing` (no bid price), `quote` (vendor-quoted scope: the CaptiveAire hood package, foodservice equipment by model, LED strip and decorative fixtures), `none_required` (quantity 0 or covered by another line, stated).

## Judgment calls to review

- **Lighting priced from the book (E-101, E-102, E-106, E-123..E-127, 51,929 substitute).** The rule applied: a fixture is priced where the book has the same kind of fixture, otherwise quote. B1 (Gotham EVO 4" wall-mounted accent cylinder, 33 EA) was priced with the only verified wall-mounted LED fixture row (66 W indoor/outdoor, 464.02 Ea.) - 15,313, the single largest substitute component of the MEP file; if the architectural accent cylinder is considered a decorative product like C1 / C2 / D1 it belongs in the quote tier instead. The Art Deco exit signs and emergency lights (Flamsted of Vermont, X1 / Y1) are decorative products priced with standard LED exit / twin-beam emergency rows; the recessed Isolite Y2 is priced with a surface twin-beam row. F1 2' x 2' panels are priced with the 2' x 4' LED troffer row because the 2' x 2' line is not verified.
- **W10x22 dumbwaiter beams (S-13) marked exact.** The row (05 12 23.75 W10x22) is the same member and grade; its printed basis is a 100-ton 1-2 story project with bolted connections, here 3 pieces / 431 lb framed to the (E) beams. The product matches; the productivity basis does not describe a 3-piece job. Move to substitute if the basis is read as part of the product.
- **Attic floor joists (S-12) priced per LF from the takeoff notes.** The line is 1,538.12 SF; the joist length 1,157 LF comes from the takeoff notes (33 x 12.8 ft + 42 x 17.5 ft), written as factor 1157/1538.12. Material and installation rows for 16 ga x 8" joists are exact; the Sure-Board decking (not in the book) and the tracks / hangers (LF not dimensioned) are unpriced components, so the line is priced for its joists only (23,221 of the 29,086 exact direct of this file).
- **Walk-in removals (D-01, D-02) per S.F.** The row 'Food storage equipment, walk-in refrigerator/freezer' is per S.F. without saying floor or panel area; the floor areas of the takeoff (119 + 32 SF tagged; 181 SF untagged, RFI whether it is a walk-in) were used. Substitute, flagged.
- **Stair pan fill (S-07) material at 2,500 psi.** The fill strength is not stated; the 2,500 psi ready-mix row was used because A-9.10 names 2,500 psi for the other slab fills and S-0.10 triggers special inspection above 2,500 psi. Lightweight concrete is not in the book. The exit-stair fill (X-03) has no depth and only the placing labor is priced.
- **Fixture rough-ins for KEC-furnished sinks (P-006..P-016).** The fixtures themselves are quote (foodservice items by model); the book's kitchen-sink rough-in (1,455.30 Ea.) and wall-hung lavatory rough-in (1,905.41 Ea.) were applied as substitutes to the 3-comp sink, prep sink, dish table, dump sinks and hand sinks. The rough-in rows carry residential-type connections; the commercial 3" direct waste of KS-1 and the indirect wastes differ. P-065 / P-066 (equipment waste and water connections) are therefore not priced again (none_required / not_in_book) to avoid a double count.
- **Pipe insulation (P-057) split by size.** Only the 1/2" IPS line of 22 07 19.10 (1" wall) is verified; the 1" line is flagged and the 3/4", 1-1/4" and 1-1/2" lines are not in the table. The 327.2 LF line was split per its own qty_source arithmetic: 150.2 LF priced exact, 177.0 LF unpriced in five components.
- **Gas 2-1/2" (P-059) threaded instead of butt-weld.** P-1 specifies butt-weld fittings at 2-1/2" and larger; the welded 2-1/2" line is not in the table, so the threaded 2-1/2" row was used as substitute. The 1-1/2" gas line (P-061, 59 LF) is unpriced because its threaded row is flagged (the verified 1-1/2" row of 22 11 13.48 is grooved-joint pipe).
- **Electrical connections priced as devices / connections only (E-014, E-015, E-017, E-018, E-022, E-129, LV-002).** No power plan exists, so only the device or the equipment connection is priced (receptacle 15 A duplex, motor-connection flexible conduit rows, fusible safety switches); boxes, conduit, wire and circuits are unpriced components. The 208 V equipment connections use the 230 V 3-phase 3 HP motor row.
- **Air devices.** Scheduled devices A-F (16) are priced with the nearest verified register / diffuser rows (sizes differ, written in reason) except C (14" x 14" line machine_read). The 23 untagged outlets drawn on M-2 (M-040) and the 5 attic symbols (M-050) are spec_not_issued; pricing them would double count the schedule.
- **Statuses beyond the task list.** `spec_not_issued` (10 rows across the files) and `none_required` (8 rows) were kept from the first estimate's status set: the former for lines that have a quantity but no product / construction to choose a row with (D-08, D-10, D-21, E-011, M-040, M-050, P-051, P-055, S-15, P-057 part), the latter for quantity-0 lines and for P-065 whose connections are inside priced rough-ins.
- **Equipment file.** All 82 foodservice items are `quote` (list prices being collected by another agent), 15 owner / vendor items `owner_provided`, the existing water heater `existing`. `candidate_row` names a verified book row of the same kind where one exists (range, fryer, broiler, ice maker, bins, dishwasher hood per LF, 3-compartment pot sink per LF, walk-in box, galvanized shelving, slicer, coffee / tea brewers) for a later install-labor or budget check.
- **Table gaps that drive the unpriced total.** No-hub cast iron soil pipe (p512, flagged, no cost code), grease duct and fire wrap (not in the book), WSHP rows (machine_read), kitchen hoods (not in the book), floor sinks and trap primers (not in the book), floor drains / backflow 3/4" / grease interceptor (flagged), galvanized ductwork by weight (duct depths not printed), chemical anchors 1/2" (not in the table), HSS 4x4 (not in the book), Sure-Board (not in the book).

## Method notes

- Quantities are those of the takeoff2 files, unchanged; where a book unit needs a conversion the factor is an arithmetic expression whose source is written in parentheses (parsed and re-printed in `unit_check`).
- Conversions used: HSS 8x4 pieces 1/12 per LF (piece length printed on the row); plate S.F. from the plate sizes on S-3.10 / S-3.20; stair pan fill 2/12/27 CY per SF (A-6.12 depth); lift pit 3.5/12/27 (A-6.21 depth); walk-in S.F. from the takeoff floor areas; joist LF 1157/1538.12 and exit-stair wall S.F. 1595/95.54 and 797/95.54 from the takeoff notes; insulation and electrical shares from the takeoff qty_source counts.
- Every priced row records cost_code, ref_line, book_page, description_path, book unit, unit_check, factor, book_qty, latest_period and the five LA latest-month unit costs; `price2.py` asserts the code is carried by exactly one verified row, that it has a unit and a total, that M + L + E = T, that book_qty reproduces the extension, that every takeoff line is in the output and that every unpriced row has a status and a reason.
- No cost code used is carried by more than one row of the table (no duplicate-code note was raised).
- Regenerate: `python3 -I scripts/build_matches2.py && for f in mep equipment demo_struct_gc; do python3 -I scripts/price2.py $f.csv matches_$f.csv <book csv> priced_$f.csv; done && python3 -I scripts/summarize2.py` from the takeoff2 folder.

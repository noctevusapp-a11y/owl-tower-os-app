- **California DIR general prevailing wage determinations: publication cycle** — General determinations are issued twice a year (February 22 and August 22) and become effective 10 days after issuance; they remain in effect until their expiration date or until modified, corrected, rescinded or superseded. Editions are published in folders named YYYY-1 and YYYY-2 (e.g. 2026-1, 2026-2) with HTML index pages and per-craft PDFs. Between editions DIR issues modification/correction notices (e.g. Aug 27 2026 plumber modification; Sep 3 2026 apprentice painter correction) and predetermined-increase files. A single asterisk after the expiration date means rates in effect when bids are advertised remain for the life of the project; double asterisk means post-expiration rates are predetermined.  
  _verification:_ `search_snippet_only` · [evidence](https://dir.ca.gov/t8/16204.html)

### Three most consequential corrections

1. Ready-mix concrete PPI and the FRED/BLS identifier hygiene. WPU1333 (NSA, 1982=100) and WPU133301 are the same ready-mix series; WPS1333 is a seasonally adjusted twin whose last value seen is Sep 2025 (stale); WPS133301 could not be found; the register title contains 'PCU3273232732', which appeared nowhere (likely a typo for PCU327320327320). ECICONWAG is a FRED-style alias, not a BLS-format series ID, which breaks the plan to 'ingest from BLS directly'.
2. Manufacturer data point contradiction: Dunn-Edwards EVEREST VOC '2 g/L' comes from an older data sheet; the Oct 2024 sheet (EVER10-EN-PDS-1024.pdf) says <5 g/L (RAVOC <3 g/L). Every manufacturer value needs the PDS revision date stored with it.
3. Terms/licensing statuses that are wrong or overstated: Grainger DOES have a robots.txt (blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot and others site-wide); the Ferguson url in the register is a product page, not the terms page; Home Depot main-site terms were still not found (only sister-site scraping bans); CSI MasterFormat is 'licence needed for a commercial derivative product', not simply 'prohibited'; the Caltrans 'PDF plus CSV' download claim is unsupported (only PDFs found).
